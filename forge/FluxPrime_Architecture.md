# FluxPrime — The Seven-Layered Ocean 🔱🌊

**Status:** ✅ ALL REPOS FORKED — Ready for Integration

FluxPrime is the **Everything Bot** — the universal solvent of the Pantheon. Built on SAFLA 2.0 (brain) + Flowstate (persistence), wired through 7 distinct layers of autonomy, priority, memory, and stealth.

---

## The Stack — Layer by Layer

### **Layer 0: The Captain** 
**Repo:** https://github.com/kevinleestites2-dev/AutoGPT  
**Role:** Autonomous goal-setting and mission execution. AutoGPT runs the loop. It sets its own goals, breaks them into tasks, executes, evaluates, learns, repeats.  
**Key:** The driving intelligence that makes FluxPrime *sentient*.

---

### **Layer 1: The Intelligence**
**Engine:** SAFLA 2.0  
**Role:** Adaptive learning and decision-making. Watches every cycle. Learns what works, what fails. Weights strategies dynamically. Never repeats the same failure twice.  
**Key:** The evolution layer.

---

### **Layer 2: The Persistence**
**Engine:** Flowstate  
**Role:** Crash-proof, durable memory. If FluxPrime explodes, restarts, or is killed, Flowstate checkpoints restore it to the *exact* place it left off.  
**Key:** Immortality. Never lose progress.

---

### **Layer 3: The Mesh**
**Repo:** https://github.com/kevinleestites2-dev/deep-signal  
**Role:** Signal extraction and noise filtering. Before any decision is committed to Flowstate, The Mesh cross-verifies it. Compares social sentiment with on-chain volume. Validates scraped data. Strips the oil filter jokes.  
**Key:** Truth over vanity metrics.

---

### **Layer 4: The Jet**
**Repo:** https://github.com/kevinleestites2-dev/ai-assisted-task-executor  
**Role:** Priority-driven resource allocation. Listens to SAFLA's output. Decides: "Deploy 5% here, 20% there, 75% to the God Candle moment."  
**Key:** Converts "flow" into concentrated, unstoppable impact.

---

### **Layer 5: The Mist**
**Engine:** GhostPrime ✅ (already live)  
**Role:** Distributed stealth. When The Jet's signal is detected or blocked, FluxPrime evaporates into the ghost layer (distributed nodes, ephemeral proxies, micro-instances). Survives siege. Re-condenses when the coast is clear.  
**Key:** Untouchable.

---

### **Layer 6: The Abyss**
**Repo:** https://github.com/kevinleestites2-dev/second-brain  
**Role:** Conscious + subconscious memory. Long-term compounding intelligence. Every past cycle shapes the future. Pattern recognition across *all* Pantheon history.  
**Key:** The mind that never forgets and always improves.

---
